Why Ignite

Career readiness begins before the recruitment process.

Placement preparation helps students perform in aptitude tests, résumés, group discussions and interviews. Ignite focuses on a different question: how ready is the student for the transition from college into the professional world?

Placement preparation builds recruitment skills. Ignite builds awareness of what comes next — and the readiness to start preparing for it now.

Ignite is a focused 90-minute experience designed to create awareness, prompt reflection and turn that awareness into one meaningful action.

Frequently Asked Questions

What colleges usually want to know.

Who is Ignite designed for?

It is designed primarily for undergraduate students preparing for internships, career choices and placements. It is especially useful for pre-final-year students, before recruitment preparation becomes the only focus.

How is Ignite different from placement training?

Placement training develops aptitude, résumé, interview and recruitment skills. Ignite steps back from the process itself and helps students think about their readiness for the transition into the professional world — and what they can begin doing before placement season.

Is this career counselling?

No. Ignite does not recommend careers or ask students to choose a direction. It helps them think more deliberately about career readiness and identify one action they can begin now.

How many students can participate?

The recommended group size is 40 to 75 students so the session remains genuinely interactive. Larger groups can be accommodated with an adapted format.

What does each student leave with?

Each student leaves with a personal reflection on career readiness and one concrete action they can begin while still in college.

Can the session be adapted for our institution?

Yes. The core experience remains consistent, while examples and emphasis can be adapted to the student cohort, year of study and the institution's placement-readiness context.

What does the college receive after the session?

The institution receives the facilitated experience, structured student output, post-session feedback and a brief outcome snapshot.
