APPROACHING WORK · IGNITE

Getting placed is one milestone. Being ready to work well is another.

Ignite is a practical college experience that helps students approaching placements practise judgement, articulation and readiness for the professional world.

THE GAP

Placement preparation helps you get the opportunity. Career readiness helps you navigate it.

Students can know how to prepare a CV or answer an interview question and still feel unprepared for what happens after the offer.

Ignite makes that gap tangible through realistic workplace situations, reflection and practice. The work is about what you bring, how you explain it and how you respond when there is no obvious answer.

WORKSHOP FORMAT

90 minutes designed to create movement.

Facilitator-led learning, individual reflection and student interaction, designed primarily for pre-final and final-year students preparing for placements and the transition into professional life.

DURATION

90 minutes

MODE

In person in college

COHORT SIZE

Maximum 75 students

FORMAT

Interactive · facilitated

WHAT STUDENTS PRACTISE

Real situations. Reflection. One next step.

The session turns career readiness from an abstract idea into something students can actually work through.

01

Recognise what you bring

Find evidence in your own experiences rather than relying on generic claims.

02

Communicate with evidence

Practise turning experience into clear articulation that another person can understand.

03

Practise judgement

Work through realistic situations involving pressure, mistakes, ownership and response.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What colleges usually want to know.

A few practical details before you decide whether Ignite fits your student cohort.

Who is Ignite designed for?

Ignite is designed primarily for pre-final and final-year undergraduate students preparing for placements and the transition into the professional world.

How is Ignite different from placement training?

Placement training develops aptitude, technical, résumé, interview and recruitment skills. Ignite complements this by helping students understand and communicate what they bring with evidence, while also reflecting on the behaviours they will need once they enter the professional world.

Is this career counselling?

No. Ignite does not recommend careers or ask students to choose a direction. It helps them think more deliberately about career readiness and identify one action they can begin now.

How many students can participate?

Ignite is limited to a maximum of 75 students per session so the experience remains genuinely interactive. For larger cohorts, multiple sessions can be scheduled.

What does each student leave with?

Each student leaves with an evidence-backed example for placement conversations, a personal reflection on career readiness, and one action they choose to take by a date they set for themselves.

What does the college receive after the session?

The institution receives the facilitated experience, structured student output, post-session feedback and a brief outcome snapshot.
