Why take Student Compass?

Many students have questions about their future:

What am I good at?

What should I explore?

How do I make better decisions?

How do I move forward?

Student Compass helps you pause and reflect on these questions.

After completing Student Compass, you will receive a reflection to help you understand where you are today and what you may want to strengthen next.
