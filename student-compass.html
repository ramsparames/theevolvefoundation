A USEFUL DIRECTION, NOT A DESTINATION
A MOMENT TO PAUSE

There is no need to have it all figured out.

Student Compass is simply a chance to pause and notice where you are today. Your reflection will give you a useful picture of where you are and one practical next step.

It won't tell you what career to choose. It will help you think about what might be useful to do next.

WHAT YOU'LL LEAVE WITH

A clearer sense of where you are today and one thing you can do next.
