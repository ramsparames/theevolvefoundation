BEFORE COLLEGE · COMPASS

Choosing a course and college is a decision. It does not have to be a rushed one.

Compass is a guided decision-making experience for students approaching college who want to understand what matters to them, examine their real options and make a choice they can explain.

THE PROBLEM

At 17 or 18, you are often asked to make a big decision before you have had much space to think about what you want.

Choosing well is not the same as choosing perfectly. Compass slows the decision down just enough to make it more thoughtful.

It does not rank careers or tell a student which college is best. It helps them define what they are looking for and bring the actual options into the conversation.

THE JOURNEY

Start with yourself. End with a decision you can explain.

Four steps keep the experience grounded in the real choice.

01

Know Me

Notice what you enjoy, what you seem to bring and what matters to you.

02

What Am I Looking For?

Define what you want from your course and your college experience.

03

Explore My Options

Bring the real courses and colleges you are considering into the conversation.

04

Check the Fit

Look at what fits, what does not and what you still need to find out.

THE OUTPUT

Not a score. A clearer way to think.

My Compass brings together what matters to the student, what they are considering, what they still need to find out, what they are leaning towards and what they will do next.

Your Child's Compass gives parents a useful summary without turning the decision into a recommendation.

The point is a better conversation: one where the student can explain the decision and the parent can listen before deciding for them.
