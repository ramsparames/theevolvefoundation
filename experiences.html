THE EXPERIENCES

Different moments ask different questions.

The Evolve Foundation does not offer one programme for every young person. Each experience is built around a different decision, a different stage and a different kind of next step.

FOUR DISTINCT EXPERIENCES

Start with the question you are actually facing.

They are not different versions of the same idea. The problem changes, so the experience changes.

WHY THE DISTINCTION MATTERS

Same philosophy. Different job to do.

Understanding yourself matters throughout the journey. What changes is what a young person needs that understanding to help them do.

ExperienceThe specific jobWhat the person leaves with
CompassMake a consequential education choice more thoughtfully.A decision they can explain and questions they still need answered.
LaunchpadTurn uncertainty in college into a direction worth testing.A chosen direction and a personalised 30-day action plan.
IgniteMake career readiness tangible before entering work.Evidence, articulation, workplace judgement and one practical next step.
MomentumBuild capability for the unwritten situations of early career.Better questions, choices, communication and ways of working.
