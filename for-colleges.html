FOR COLLEGES

Prepare students for the opportunity — and for what comes after.

Meaningful student development can complement placement preparation.

Ignite is a 90-minute, interactive, in-person experience for pre-final and final-year students. It helps students recognise what they bring, communicate it with evidence, and think about how they will show up in the professional world.

IGNITE

Awareness. Readiness. Action.

Ignite adds a practical layer beyond aptitude, technical, résumé and interview preparation.

01 · PLACEMENT READINESS

Get ready to get the opportunity.

Recognise what you bring.

Students find evidence in their own experiences and practise communicating it through familiar placement questions and conversations.

02 · CAREER READINESS

Get ready for the opportunity.

Think beyond the offer.

Students work through realistic early-career situations and reflect on communication, ownership, initiative, collaboration, adaptability and learning.

03 · ACTION

Make the next move.

Each student identifies one area to strengthen, chooses one meaningful action and sets a date by which they will complete it.

WHAT STUDENTS TOLD US
  • Beyond placement. Students distinguished getting the job from being ready to work well once they get there.
  • Real workplace judgement. Mistakes, pressure, ownership and how to respond in real situations were memorable.
  • Articulation matters. Students valued learning how to turn experiences into evidence and explain them clearly in interviews.
  • Action over intention. Several comments focused on practice, upskilling, consistency and taking one small step.
