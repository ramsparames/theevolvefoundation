EARLY COLLEGE · LAUNCHPAD

Launchpad turns reflection into direction and a practical next step.

Self Awareness

Direction

Confidence

APPROACHING WORK · IGNITE

Four concrete outputs from the experience.

Ignite adds a practical layer beyond aptitude, technical, résumé and interview preparation.

01

Placement evidence

An evidence-backed example students can use in placement conversations.

02

Clear articulation

Students practise articulating their experiences clearly.

03

Career Readiness Snapshot

A structured snapshot of what they have understood and where they can strengthen.

04

One action

One personally chosen action and a completion date.
