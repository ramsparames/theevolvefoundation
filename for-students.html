FOR STUDENTS

You don't need to have everything figured out.

College is a time to explore, make choices and learn what fits. You don't have to know the whole path before you take the next step.

The Evolve Foundation creates experiences and resources that give you space to pause, reflect, understand yourself better and think more deliberately about what comes next.

START WITH STUDENT COMPASS

Not sure what comes next?

Student Compass is a simple 10 minute reflection to help you pause, think about yourself, and notice what may be worth exploring next.

No right or wrong answers. Just honest reflection.

Take Student Compass →

What will you get?

Your Compass Reflection. Understand areas where you feel confident and areas where you may want to grow.

Ideas to Explore. Discover ideas and resources that may help you think further.

Your Next Step. Identify one area you may want to focus on as you continue your journey.

GO DEEPER

When you want more than a reflection.

Launchpad is the Foundation's deeper 8 week experience for college students who want the time and space to understand themselves, make thoughtful choices and move forward with a direction they can own.

LAUNCHPAD · 8 WEEK LIVE ONLINE COHORT

Clarity. Confidence. Direction.

Understand yourself. Think through your choices. Decide what to test next.

Over eight weeks, students identify their proven strengths, choose one direction worth testing, practise explaining that direction with confidence and leave with a dated 30 day action plan.
