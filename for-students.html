START WITH YOUR QUESTION

Which of these sounds most like you?

You are not choosing a programme for the rest of your life. You are choosing support for the decision you are facing now.

BEFORE COLLEGE

“I have options. How do I know what actually fits me?”

Compass helps you look at what matters to you and the real courses and colleges you are considering.

Explore Compass →

ALREADY IN COLLEGE

“I have interests, but I do not know what direction to test.”

Launchpad gives you eight weeks to reflect, make a choice and turn it into a 30-day action plan.

Explore Launchpad →

APPROACHING WORK

“I can prepare for placements. But will I know how to work?”

Ignite helps you practise articulation, judgement and workplace situations before you enter them.

Explore Ignite →

AND IF YOU ARE JUST THINKING

REAL STUDENTS. REAL QUESTIONS.

The questions these experiences are designed around are already being asked.

At PSG College of Technology, approximately 150 students spent time with Rams discussing college, career choices and what comes next. Launchpad was introduced as one way to create more structured space for those questions.
