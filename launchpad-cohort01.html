THE COHORT

A first cohort with different questions, interests and starting points.

Founding Cohort 01 has now completed its eight week journey. Six college students came into Launchpad with different questions, different interests and different levels of clarity about what might come next.

At the end of the programme, we asked them to reflect on what felt different.

A REFLECTION FROM RAMS

The most meaningful outcome was seeing students become more comfortable thinking for themselves.

We did not set out to give them answers. We created the space to pause, reflect, question assumptions, make choices with the information they had and take those choices forward.

For a first cohort, that was the most meaningful outcome I could have hoped for.
