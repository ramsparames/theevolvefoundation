Launchpad · Cohort 01

Eight weeks. Six students. A first Launchpad journey.

Founding Cohort 01 has now completed its eight week journey. Six college students came into Launchpad with different questions, different interests and different levels of clarity about what might come next. At the end of the programme, we asked them to reflect on what felt different.

8 weeks·6 students·One journey
“This programme was like a source of light in the dark tunnel of ignorance.”
Balasubramanyam Baranitharan · Vivekananda College · 1st Year
“More in control of my own life. Confident with my decisions and have a clear futuristic plan to look forward to.”
Dharshini A · Rajalakshmi Engineering College · 3rd Year
“I started to set an achievable goal without overthinking.”
Sujan Anand · VIT Vellore · 1st Year
A reflection from Rams

What stayed with me most from Cohort 01 was seeing students become more comfortable with the process of thinking for themselves. We did not set out to give them answers. We created the space to pause, reflect, question assumptions, make choices with the information they had and take those choices forward. For a first cohort, that was the most meaningful outcome I could have hoped for.
