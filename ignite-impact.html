APPROACHING WORK · IGNITE · RAJALAKSHMI ENGINEERING COLLEGE

What happened when students experienced Ignite.

A look at the first Ignite session at Rajalakshmi Engineering College, what students told us afterwards, and what they found most useful.

Rajalakshmi Engineering College 24 September 2026 Third-year students across engineering disciplines

THE SESSION

Real situations. Reflection. One next step.

At Rajalakshmi Engineering College, about 80 third-year students experienced Ignite as a practical bridge between placement preparation and career readiness.

Students worked through realistic workplace situations, practised turning their experiences into evidence, and reflected on what career readiness means beyond getting placed.

~80Students attended
4.65 / 5Average usefulness
98%Rated Ignite 4 or 5
86%Likely / very likely to act on their chosen next step

WHAT STUDENTS TOLD US

What stayed with them.

Beyond placement.

Getting the job is different from being ready to work well once you get there.

Real workplace judgement.

Students connected with mistakes, pressure, ownership and how to respond in real situations.

Articulation matters.

Students valued learning how to turn their experiences into evidence and explain them clearly.

Action matters.

The session encouraged practice, consistency and one small next step.

IN THEIR WORDS

What students took away.

“How to work with real world experiences.”
“I have always struggled to articulate, but after this session, I feel quite confident of building my articulation skills.”
“How to face the interview and how to be ready after getting the job.”
“How to handle real workplace situations.”

WHAT STUDENTS VALUED MOST

The parts they found most useful.

THE TAKEAWAY

Placement preparation helps you get the opportunity. Career readiness helps you navigate it well.
