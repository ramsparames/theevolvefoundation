IGNITE · IN ACTION

What happened when students experienced Ignite.

A look at the first Ignite session at Rajalakshmi Engineering College, what students told us afterwards, and what they found most useful.

Rajalakshmi Engineering College · 24 September 2026 · Third-year students across engineering disciplines

RAJALAKSHMI ENGINEERING COLLEGE

A practical bridge between placement preparation and career readiness.

The first session brought together about 80 nominated students and used real workplace situations, two simple frameworks and reflection exercises to move from awareness to action.

~80Students attended
43Feedback responses
4.65 / 5Average usefulness
98%Rated Ignite 4 or 5
98%Better understood career readiness beyond placement
86%Likely / very likely to act on their chosen next step
THE SESSION

Real situations. Reflection. One next step.

Students worked through realistic workplace situations, practised turning their experiences into evidence, and reflected on what career readiness means beyond getting placed.

The strongest responses clustered around using experience as evidence, articulating it clearly and working through realistic workplace situations.

Ignite session · Rajalakshmi Engineering College

The distinction landed.
