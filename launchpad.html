THE THINKING BEHIND LAUNCHPAD

College is full of choices. Students need space to make choices they can own.

Launchpad is built on a simple belief: college students make better choices when they have space to understand themselves, test a direction and learn from doing.

It is not about finding one perfect answer. It is about helping students understand what matters to them, make a thoughtful choice and take a first step to test it.

Understand yourself. Think through your choices. Decide what is worth testing next.

WHY LAUNCHPAD

Three challenges that often sit underneath “I don't know what I want.”

These are not shortcomings. They are common challenges that come with having more choices and more pressure to make the right decision.

DIRECTION

Finding Real Direction

Big decisions are often shaped by family expectations or peer pressure because students rarely get structured space to explore what they themselves actually want.

CONFIDENCE

Building Real Confidence

Confidence takes more than interview tips. It grows when a student understands what they are good at and has real examples to support it.

THE GAP

Bridging Learning and Life

Launchpad creates space to connect what students are learning with who they are becoming and the choices they will make next.

THE PROGRAMME

Four modules. Eight weeks.

Each module builds on what students discovered and wrote in the previous one. This is not a lecture series.

01 · WEEKS 1–2

Self Awareness

A clearer picture of who they are, with strengths backed by real examples and feedback from people who know them well.

02 · WEEKS 3–4

Clarity

The fear underneath avoidance gets named. A direction gets chosen because it connects to a real strength, not just excitement.

03 · WEEKS 5–6

Decision & Confidence

Direction becomes a real decision. Students practise explaining what they chose, why they chose it and what they will do next.

04 · WEEKS 7–8

Real World Readiness

A plan for when things get difficult, one small habit and a dated 30-day action plan built gradually across the programme.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What students usually want to know.

Clear answers before you register for Cohort 02.

Who is Launchpad for?

Launchpad is for college students in India who want greater clarity about their strengths, direction and next steps, and who are willing to participate actively. Students can join from any year and any stream, including engineering, commerce, arts and science.

Is this career counselling or a psychometric test?

No. Launchpad does not prescribe a career or use a test to assign one. It is a facilitated eight-week developmental programme in which students identify their proven strengths, choose one direction worth testing and leave with a dated 30-day action plan they have written themselves.

How much work is expected between sessions?

Usually 10–15 minutes each week. It may involve answering one question, having one conversation or taking one small action in the real world. The intention is to help students reflect and apply what they learn, not to make it feel like homework.

What if a student is introverted?

Sharing is never forced. Sessions combine individual reflection, pair conversations and voluntary group discussion, allowing different personalities to participate comfortably.

Are sessions recorded?

Personal group discussions are generally not recorded. This gives students the space to speak honestly and participate comfortably. Students are also asked to respect the privacy of others in the cohort.

Do parents attend the sessions?

No. The sessions are the student's space, which is important for honest reflection and independent thinking. Parents receive a short note at the beginning explaining the programme and how they can support the process.
