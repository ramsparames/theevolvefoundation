03 · YOUR OPTIONS

Now we have a real decision to work with.

One course or direction. Up to three colleges. Next, Compass will use your earlier priorities to help you investigate the fit rather than simply repeating your choices back to you.

THE POINT OF THIS STEP

We are separating the course decision from the college decision.

A college can be excellent overall and still be a poor fit for a particular course or student. Step 4 is where we start checking that difference.

Now let's check the fit.We will look at course strength, practical exposure, opportunities, environment, career path and cost.
04 CHECK THE FIT Evidence, not guesses

04 · YOUR FIT CHECK

Now you can see where the evidence is strong — and where it is missing.

This is the part that turns “I like this college” into a more useful decision process: what is supported, what is uncertain and what needs verification.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Unknown does not mean bad.

It means you have found a question worth answering. The final Compass will turn those gaps into a short list of next actions rather than pretending the information is already known.

Now pull it together.Compass will show you what the decision looks like today and what could make it clearer.

05 · MY COMPASS
