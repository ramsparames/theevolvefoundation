A Note to Parents

You do not have to choose your child's path. You can help them learn how to choose.

Parents often see their child's potential before the child can put it into words. Launchpad gives college students aged 18 to 22 a structured and professionally facilitated space to understand their strengths, explore possible directions and take ownership of their next step without feeling that they must have their entire future figured out.

Your role remains important. We encourage supportive conversations at home while making sure that the college student's thinking, decisions and action plan remain their own.

What changes

More specific conversations

Instead of 'I don't know,' college students begin explaining what they are good at, what they want to test, and why.

What they leave with

Visible, written outcomes

A validated strengths list, one direction worth testing, a practised personal story and a dated 30 day plan.

How it is delivered

Small group and individual support

Eight live sessions in a cohort of 15 to 20 college students, along with direct one to one time with the facilitator.

What it is not

Not a test that assigns a career

Launchpad does not prescribe one 'correct' profession. It helps college students make a grounded decision and test it through action.

Four waypoints. Eight weeks. One plotted course.

This is not a lecture series. Each module builds on what college students discovered and wrote in the previous one.

WK 01 TO 02

Self Awareness

A clearer picture of who they are, with strengths backed by real examples and feedback from people who know them well.

WK 03 TO 04

Clarity

The fear underneath avoidance gets named; a direction gets chosen because it connects to a real strength, not just excitement.

WK 05 TO 06

Decision & Confidence

Direction becomes a real decision. College students also practise explaining what they chose, why they chose it and what they will do next.

WK 07 TO 08

Real World Readiness

A plan for when things get difficult, one small habit and a dated 30 day action plan built gradually across the programme.

ITEM 5: BEYOND LAUNCHPAD

Who is Launchpad for?

College students aged 18 to 22 who want greater clarity about their strengths, direction and next steps, and who are willing to participate actively.

Is this career counselling?

No. Launchpad does not prescribe a career or use a test to assign one. It helps college students understand themselves, choose a direction worth testing and act on it.

What if a college student is introverted?

Sharing is never forced. Sessions combine individual reflection, pair conversations and voluntary group discussion so different personalities can participate comfortably.

What if a college student misses a session?

We provide the essential technique, worked example and reflection prompt so one absence does not prevent a college student from continuing with the cohort.

How much work is expected between sessions?

Usually 10 to 15 minutes. It may be one question, one conversation or one small action in the real world. It is meant to help college students reflect, not feel like homework.

Are sessions recorded?

Personal group discussions are generally not recorded, so college students can speak honestly in a psychologically safe setting.
