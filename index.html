THE EVOLVE FOUNDATION

Helping young people understand themselves and navigate life well.

We create thoughtful experiences for young people aged 17 to 25 at different moments in their lives, from choosing what to study, to finding direction in college, to preparing for work and learning how to navigate it.

OUR CORE PHILOSOPHY

We do not tell young people who they should become.

We help them understand themselves well enough to make thoughtful decisions about who they want to become.

Young adulthood is not one moment. The questions change as a young person moves from school to college to work. We create experiences for those different moments, without pretending there is one right path.

DIFFERENT MOMENTS. DIFFERENT QUESTIONS.

There isn't one path through young adulthood.

The right support depends on the decision a young person is facing now.

WHY THE EVOLVE FOUNDATION EXISTS

Experience showed me that knowing yourself matters.

For nearly 30 years, Rams Parameswaran built and led technology organisations across the United States and India, working with global financial institutions including Citigroup, Bank of America, BNY Mellon and Fiserv.

Throughout his career, he hired, coached and mentored professionals and saw the same pattern repeatedly: those who thrived understood their strengths, could explain their choices with confidence and took ownership of their direction.
