The Evolve Foundation

Helping young people understand themselves and navigate life well.

The Evolve Foundation is an educational initiative for young people aged 17 to 24. We create meaningful learning experiences that help them understand themselves, develop confidence and judgement, and navigate life’s opportunities and challenges well.

Our Core Philosophy
We do not tell young people who they should become. We help them understand themselves well enough to make thoughtful decisions about who they want to become.

Young adulthood is not one moment. Between 17 and 24, young people may be preparing to enter college, finding their way through it, graduating, or taking their first steps into work and adult life. The Evolve Foundation exists to create thoughtful experiences for those transitions.

Why The Evolve Foundation Exists

Experience showed me that knowing yourself matters.

For nearly 30 years, Rams Parameswaran built and led technology organisations across the United States and India, working with global financial institutions including Citigroup, Bank of America, BNY Mellon and Fiserv.

Throughout his career, he hired, coached and mentored professionals and saw the same pattern repeatedly: those who thrived understood their strengths, could explain their choices with confidence and took ownership of their direction.

Rams founded The Evolve Foundation to help young people develop that self-understanding earlier.
