FOR PARENTS

Support your child through the decisions that shape what comes next.

From choosing a college and course to finding direction once college begins, the right support changes with the moment.

The Evolve Foundation offers two different experiences for two different stages: Compass for students approaching college and Launchpad for students already in college.

BEFORE COLLEGE · COMPASS

Choosing well starts before choosing a course.

Compass is a guided digital experience for students who are still deciding what to study and where to study. It helps them understand themselves, think through what matters and make a more informed decision, with a parent report at the end.

Explore Compass →
THE PARENT'S ROLE

Support the decision without making it for them.

Compass gives students and parents a shared starting point for a more useful conversation about strengths, interests, options and what still needs to be explored.

Compass is for the decision before college.

It is not a career test or a recommendation engine. It is a structured decision-making experience designed to help students and parents think more clearly together.

EARLY COLLEGE · LAUNCHPAD

Once college begins, the question changes.

Launchpad is an eight-week live, online experience for college students who want to understand themselves better, think through their choices and decide what direction they want to test next.
