TWO MOMENTS. TWO EXPERIENCES.

The support your child needs changes with the stage they are in.

The Evolve Foundation offers Compass for students approaching college and Launchpad for students in early college.

APPROACHING COLLEGE · COMPASS

Choosing well starts before choosing a course.

Compass is a guided digital experience for students who are still deciding what to study and where to study. It helps them understand themselves, think through what matters and make a more informed decision, with a parent report at the end.

Explore Compass →

EARLY COLLEGE · LAUNCHPAD

Once college begins, the question changes.

Launchpad is an eight-week live, online experience for college students who want to understand themselves better, think through their choices and decide what direction they want to test next.

Explore Launchpad →

WHAT YOUR CHILD WILL GET

Each experience leaves them with something they can use.

Compass helps with the decision approaching college. Launchpad helps students in early college turn reflection into direction and action.

APPROACHING COLLEGE · COMPASS

A clearer way to make the decision.

01

My Compass

What matters to me, what I am considering, what I still need to find out and what I will do next.

02

Your Child's Compass

A parent-friendly summary of what the student is considering and where questions remain.

03

A conversation worth having

Questions that help students and parents discuss the decision without one person simply making it for the other.

EARLY COLLEGE · LAUNCHPAD

Four things they can take forward.

01

Self Awareness

Understand strengths through reflection, examples and feedback.

02

Direction

Choose one direction worth exploring rather than trying to solve everything at once.

03

Confidence

Communicate what they bring with greater clarity.

04

Action

Turn the thinking into a practical next step they can own.
