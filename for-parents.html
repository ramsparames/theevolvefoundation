FOR PARENTS

You do not have to choose your child's path.

You can help them learn how to choose.

College is an exciting time, but it can also be uncertain for a young person. Students face more choices than ever before, yet very few opportunities to pause and understand themselves before making important decisions.

THE CHALLENGE

I don't know is often not the whole story.

Students may be dealing with too many choices, fear of making the wrong decision, comparison with peers or pressure to have everything figured out.

Launchpad creates structured space for them to understand their strengths, examine possible directions and take ownership of their next step without pressure to have their entire future figured out.

01

Self Awareness

A clearer picture of who they are, with strengths backed by real examples and feedback from people who know them well.

02

Clarity

Explore what could be worth testing before they make a decision.

03

Decision & Confidence

Make a choice they can explain and feel confident moving forward.

04

Real World Readiness

Turn their thinking into a practical plan and take the next step.

LAUNCHPAD

An eight week experience for college students.

Launchpad is not here to tell your child what career to choose. It is a facilitated developmental programme that helps them make a thoughtful decision for themselves.

WHAT YOUR CHILD WILL LEAVE WITH

Visible outcomes

Clarity. Confidence. Direction.

A clearer understanding of their strengths, greater confidence communicating who they are, one direction worth testing and a personalised 30 day action plan.

HOW IT WORKS

Small group attention

Live. Interactive. Personal.

Eight live sessions in a cohort of 15 to 20 students, with reflection, conversations and practical activities, plus direct one to one time with the facilitator.

YOUR ROLE AS A PARENT

Curiosity builds confidence better than pressure.

We encourage supportive conversations at home while ensuring the student's thinking, decisions and action plan remain genuinely their own.

Try asking

What surprised you about yourself this week?
What did you learn about yourself?
What's one small step you'll take next?

WHAT LAUNCHPAD IS NOT

Not another test that assigns a career.

Launchpad is not career counselling, placement coaching, personality testing or therapy.

It is guided self discovery, confidence building, better decision making, structured reflection and practical action.
