Ideas to Explore

Continue your reflection.

A few simple ideas to help you keep thinking about yourself, your choices and what may be worth trying next.

01

Fear cannot drive the bus

Notice when fear is making a decision for you, and separate the feeling from the direction you actually want to explore.

02

The Regret Test

For a decision worth testing, imagine looking back later. Which choice would you regret not trying?

03

Confidence is knowing why you chose

You do not need certainty to make a useful decision. You need to understand why you are choosing it with what you know today.
