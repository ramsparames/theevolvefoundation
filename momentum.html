EARLY CAREER · MOMENTUM

The first few years of work come without a manual.

Momentum is a six-workshop experience for early-career professionals learning to take ownership, use judgement and communicate clearly when the rules are rarely written down.

THE PROBLEM

You can be good at your job and still not know how to handle the moments around the job.

Who speaks up? When do you push back? What do you do when the brief is unclear? How do you handle disagreement without making it personal?

Momentum is built around those situations. It is not generic “professional skills” training; it is practice for the judgement calls that make early career difficult.

SIX WORKSHOP EXPERIENCES

Two core capabilities. Six practical experiences.

The six workshops are built around taking ownership when there is no manual, and using your judgement when the situation calls for it. Each workshop gives participants a chance to practise, reflect and apply.

01

Operate Without a Manual

How to find your way when the answer isn't written down.

02

Speak Up

How to make your point, ask for what you need and handle disagreement.

03

Make Your Work Count

How do you become visible for the right reasons? Build credibility, make your contribution visible and develop relationships that matter.

04

Build a Working Life That Works

How do you build a career without allowing work to consume you? Think about boundaries, energy, priorities and sustainable choices.

05

Use AI Thoughtfully

How to use new tools without outsourcing your judgement.

06

Personal Finance

Practical personal finance concepts worth knowing early.

WHAT STAYS

Ownership, judgement and communication.

Participants should leave better able to take ownership, build accountability for their work, use judgement when the situation is ambiguous and communicate clearly when it is difficult.
