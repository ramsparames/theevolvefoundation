MOMENTUM · EARLY CAREER

There is no manual for the first few years of work.

Momentum helps early-career professionals learn how to navigate work with more ownership, confidence and judgement.

A six-workshop experience built around real situations: taking ownership, speaking up, building credibility, making thoughtful choices, using AI intelligently and building a sound financial foundation early in a career.

THE THINKING BEHIND MOMENTUM

The first years of work are where judgement gets built.

Momentum is not designed to give early-career professionals a manual. It is designed to give them situations worth practising. The programme helps them build judgement through realistic choices, conversations and trade-offs so that confidence comes from experience rather than performance.

You will not always have a manual. You can learn how to navigate without one.
WHY MOMENTUM

The first few years of work can feel like a lot of figuring it out as you go.

Momentum makes that learning more deliberate. The sessions use simulations, how-to scenarios, practical tips and real perspectives rather than another lecture-heavy programme.

SIX WORKSHOP EXPERIENCES

Two core capabilities, six practical experiences.

The six workshops are built around two capabilities that matter early in a career: taking ownership when there is no manual, and using your judgement when the situation calls for it. Each workshop gives participants a chance to practise, reflect and apply.

01

Operate Without a Manual

How to find your way when the answer isn't written down.

02

Speak Up

How to make your point, ask for what you need and handle disagreement.

03

Make Your Work Count

How do you become visible for the right reasons? Build credibility, make your contribution visible and develop relationships that matter.

04

Build a Working Life That Works

How do you build a career without allowing work to consume you? Think about boundaries, energy, priorities and sustainable choices.

05

Use AI Thoughtfully

How to use new tools without outsourcing your judgement.

06

Personal Finance

Practical personal finance concepts worth knowing early.

WHAT PARTICIPANTS SHOULD LEAVE WITH

More than six workshops completed.

Momentum is designed to leave participants better equipped for the situations that make early work life difficult.

Ownership

A clearer sense of what to do when the steps are not given to you.

Judgement

More practice thinking through choices, asking better questions and handling disagreement constructively.

Confidence

More confidence built from practice, evidence and better decisions rather than simply feeling confident.

MOMENTUM

Help early-career professionals build the judgement to navigate work well.

Momentum is designed for organisations that want development to feel practical, relevant and connected to the situations young professionals actually face.
